常見問題

找不到路線？

請確認網路連線，並確認已選擇所需的交通方式。部分班次資料來自公開的時刻表，可能與實際營運不同。

位置無法顯示？

請到「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「定位服務」，允許 Via 使用位置。導航期間若要在螢幕鎖定時持續引導，請保持定位權限開啟。

我的資料如何處理？

請見隱私權政策。

Contact

FAQ

No routes found?

Check your connection and make sure the transport modes you need are selected. Some services come from published timetables and may differ from actual operations.

Location not showing?

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services and allow Via to use your location. To keep guidance going while the screen is locked during navigation, keep location access enabled.

How is my data handled?

See the Privacy Policy.
