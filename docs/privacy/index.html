Via 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-11-23

Via 是一款臺灣大眾運輸行程規劃 App。本政策說明 Via 會處理哪些資料，以及如何處理。

我們不做的事

  • 沒有帳號、不需登入。
  • 不追蹤你，不做跨 App 或跨網站的追蹤。
  • 不使用任何第三方分析、廣告或其他第三方 SDK。
  • 不販售或分享你的資料以作廣告用途。

位置資料

Via 在你允許時使用裝置的精確位置，用於顯示你在地圖上的位置、規劃從你所在位置出發的行程，以及在導航期間持續提供引導（包含螢幕鎖定時）。你可以隨時在「設定」中關閉位置權限。

規劃行程時，起點與終點座標會傳送到 Via 伺服器，僅用於計算行程。

Via 伺服器不保留請求中的座標：不會將座標寫入紀錄，也不會儲存你的行程歷史。

地點搜尋

地點搜尋使用 Apple 地圖（MapKit）。你輸入的搜尋文字會依 Apple 的隱私權政策由 Apple 處理，不會經過 Via 伺服器。

儲存在你裝置上的資料

你選擇的交通方式偏好與已選地點等設定只儲存在你的裝置上（UserDefaults），不會上傳。

資料來源

  • 大眾運輸資料：資料介接交通部 TDX 運輸資料流通服務平臺。
  • 街道與步行資料：© OpenStreetMap 貢獻者，依 ODbL 授權。
  • 公共自行車（YouBike）資訊：經由 TDX 取得。

兒童

Via 不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料；本 App 不需要任何個人資料即可使用。

政策變更

若本政策有重大變更，我們會更新本頁面及上方日期。

聯絡我們

如有疑問，請見支援頁面。

Via Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Via is a public transport journey planner for Taiwan. This policy explains what data Via handles and how.

What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No tracking, across apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising or other third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for advertising.

Location

With your permission, Via uses your device's precise location to show where you are on the map, to plan trips starting from your position, and to keep guiding you during navigation, including while your screen is locked. You can turn location off at any time in Settings.

When you plan a trip, the origin and destination coordinates are sent to the Via server solely to compute the trip.

The Via server does not keep request coordinates: they are not written to logs and no trip history is stored.

Place search

Place search uses Apple Maps (MapKit). The text you type is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and does not pass through the Via server.

Data stored on your device

Settings such as your selected transport modes and selected places are stored only on your device (UserDefaults) and are never uploaded.

Data sources

  • Public transport data: provided through the Ministry of Transportation and Communications TDX platform (資料介接交通部 TDX 平臺).
  • Street and walking data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the ODbL.
  • Bike-share (YouBike) information: obtained through TDX.

Children

Via does not knowingly collect personal information from children; the app needs no personal information to work.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the date above.
