Via 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-11-24

資料控管者：[Owner full name]（個人開發者）。Via 是一款臺灣大眾運輸行程規劃 App。本政策說明 Via 會處理哪些資料，以及如何處理。

我們不做的事

  • 沒有帳號、不需登入。
  • 不追蹤你，不做跨 App 或跨網站的追蹤。
  • 不使用任何第三方分析、廣告或其他第三方 SDK。
  • 不販售或分享你的資料以作廣告用途。

位置資料

Via 在你允許時使用裝置的精確位置，用於顯示你在地圖上的位置、規劃從你所在位置出發的行程，以及在導航期間持續提供引導（包含螢幕鎖定時）。你可以隨時在「設定」中關閉位置權限。

以下情況會將座標傳送到 Via 伺服器，且僅用於計算結果：

  • 規劃行程時：起點與終點的座標與顯示名稱（起點可能是你目前的位置）。
  • 查看附近站牌時：你查詢的地圖位置座標。
  • 導航期間 Via 重新規劃路線時（最多每分鐘一次）：你目前的位置與目的地。

Via 伺服器不會記錄這些座標，也不會儲存你的行程歷史。座標不會傳送給 TDX 或其他第三方；伺服器向 TDX 查詢即時資料時，只會傳送站牌與路線識別碼。路線計算使用我們自行架設的服務。

保存與刪除

座標只在記憶體中處理，回應送出後即捨棄。除下方的營運紀錄外，Via 不持有個人資料，因此沒有可供刪除或匯出的資料。

基礎設施營運紀錄會保留 IP 位址與時間戳記 7 天，其中不含座標。

地圖與地點搜尋

地圖顯示與地點搜尋使用 Apple 地圖（MapKit），由 Apple 依 Apple 的隱私權政策處理。你輸入的搜尋文字不會經過 Via 伺服器。

儲存在你裝置上的資料

你選擇的交通方式偏好與已選地點等設定只儲存在你裝置的本機，不會上傳。

資料來源

  • 大眾運輸資料：資料介接交通部 TDX 運輸資料流通服務平臺。
  • 街道與步行資料：© OpenStreetMap 貢獻者，依 ODbL 授權。
  • 公共自行車（YouBike）資訊：經由 TDX 取得。

兒童

Via 不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料；本 App 不需要任何個人資料即可使用，也沒有年齡限制內容。

政策變更

若本政策有重大變更，我們會更新本頁面及上方日期。

聯絡我們

隱私相關問題請來信：chengpong1127@gmail.com。一般問題與回報請見支援頁面。

Via Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Data controller: [Owner full name], an individual developer. Via is a public transport journey planner for Taiwan. This policy explains what data Via handles and how.

What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No tracking, across apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising or other third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for advertising.

Location

With your permission, Via uses your device's precise location to show where you are on the map, to plan trips starting from your position, and to keep guiding you during navigation, including while your screen is locked. You can turn location off at any time in Settings.

Coordinates are sent to the Via server, solely to compute the result, when:

  • you plan a trip: the origin and destination coordinates and display names (the origin may be your current location);
  • you view nearby stops: the coordinates of the map area you look up;
  • Via re-plans during navigation (at most once a minute): your current location and your destination.

The Via server does not record these coordinates and keeps no trip history. Coordinates are not sent to TDX or any other third party; when the server fetches live data from TDX it sends only stop and route identifiers. Route calculation runs on services we host ourselves.

Retention and deletion

Coordinates are processed in memory and discarded once the response is sent. Via does not hold personal data beyond these operational logs, so there is nothing else to delete or export.

Infrastructure operational logs keep your IP address and a timestamp, without coordinates, for 7 days.

Maps and place search

Apple Maps (MapKit), including map display and place search, is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The text you type into search does not pass through the Via server.

Data stored on your device

Settings such as your selected transport modes and selected places are stored only locally on your device and are never uploaded.

Data sources

  • Public transport data: provided through the Ministry of Transportation and Communications TDX platform (資料介接交通部 TDX 平臺).
  • Street and walking data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the ODbL.
  • Bike-share (YouBike) information: obtained through TDX.

Children

Via does not knowingly collect personal information from children; the app needs no personal information to work and has no age-gated content.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to chengpong1127@gmail.com. For general questions and bug reports, see the support page.
